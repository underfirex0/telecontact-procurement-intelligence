# Provider acquisition & relationship development (module 09)

Route `/acquisition` (tabs: Pipeline, Workflow, Discovery & gaps, Provider registration, Outreach templates, Meetings & follow-ups).

## A. Discovery
- **Supply–demand gap**: per sector, last-12-month tenders and known value vs suppliers, active/negotiating partners and Morocco coverage. Flags "Recruit" (≥4 tenders, no active partner) and "Thin" (≥6 tenders per active partner).
- **Providers to approach**: Identified / Not contacted providers with country, sector, availability and a "Draft intro" shortcut.

## B. Onboarding (demo registration form)
Company, country, city, sectors, services, main contact, role, email, phone, geographic coverage, website, areas of cooperation, additional information, consent checkbox. Validated; creates an "Identified" supplier + contact with a follow-up in 5 days and appears under Recent registrations.

## C. Relationship workflow (9 steps)
1 Identify → 2 Review profile → 3 Prepare introduction → 4 Record contact → 5 Record response → 6 Schedule meeting → 7 Evaluate → 8 Establish partnership → 9 Maintain. The Workflow tab shows counts per step and conversion metrics; the Pipeline tab is a board by stage with **Advance** (requires an activity record), **Meet** and **Re-activate** actions.

## Outreach (simulated)
Four templates (Introduction, Follow-up, Meeting request, Partnership proposal) merged with the provider profile and live market figures (12-month sector demand, active tenders). Drafts are editable and copyable. "Log as sent (simulated)" records the interaction, sets a follow-up and moves Identified/Not contacted providers to Contacted. **No email or external communication is implemented.**

## Meetings & follow-ups
Follow-ups due within 14 days (overdue in red) with quick log/meet actions; scheduled and past meetings.

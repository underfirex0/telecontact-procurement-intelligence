# Market intelligence (module 06)

Route `/market` · Analytics: `market.ts`, `concentration.ts`, `recurrence.ts`. Global filters: period (full, last 12 months, each year), sector, region, buyer type.

| Tab | Content |
|---|---|
| Trends | Monthly publications with average line; quarterly demand stacked by domain (five main domains + Other); growth, emerging and decline findings with evidence |
| Sectors | Count and value rankings; sector table with median value, 12-month vs prior and trend badge; rows open the filtered opportunity list |
| Regions | Regional demand with medians; regional momentum (ghost bars = prior 12 months); region × sector heatmap |
| Buyer demand | Top buyers, buyer-type share, top-5 buyer share and buyer HHI; buyer × sector heatmap |
| Value distribution | Count and value by band, median vs mean vs P90, procedure table |
| Competition & concentration | Sector HHI bars coloured by band, buyer dependence table, bidders per tender |
| Recurrence & seasonality | Recurring share and median cycle per sector; monthly seasonality index |
| Period comparison | Any two periods, by sector or region, with change |

Every chart shows n and period; explanatory sentences are computed (e.g. median vs mean gap, most active sector, peak month). The Overview reuses the same functions for its brief. The Market Intelligence Report exports the same findings.
